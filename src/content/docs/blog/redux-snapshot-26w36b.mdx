We have just released the second snapshot for week 36 of 2026.

This snapshot fixes a game-breaking issue where opening resource information in the Tracking Station could leave the game stuck on a loading screen. It also improves docking port rotation and strut behavior: struts connecting the two sections of a docked vessel now prevent rotation until they are disconnected, and struts can now be disconnected from the Parts Manager or through action groups.
