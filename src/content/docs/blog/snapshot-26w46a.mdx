This snapshot adds a Free Flight camera to the Alt-F12 debug menu, so you can fly around celestial bodies without a vessel. The VAB gets keyboard shortcuts for saving, loading, side panels and part categories, plus numpad orthographic views. R&D is easier to navigate, with clickable prerequisites, highlighted prerequisite paths and number keys to switch tiers.

Clouds look sharper and steadier when the camera moves, and they cost less to render and use less memory. Trajectory and impact predictions now follow the terrain properly, and closest approach markers appear where the encounter actually happens.

There are also a lot of bug fixes across flight, construction, missions and the interface, including SAS overshooting when using RCS, vessels jumping when switching into physics warp, and memory that was never freed when loading saves repeatedly.
