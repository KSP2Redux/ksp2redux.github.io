Time for a *spoOooOooky* Halloween snapshot!

This one's full of treats and no tricks: new SM+ parts for your monstrous creations, new symmetry controls and lots of VAB improvements to make building less of a nightmare, an in-flight Vessel Info window, and an optional performance benchmark for those brave enough to face the results!

We also exorcised some scary performance demons haunting large vessels and terrain rendering, updated ghostly atmospheres and plumes and reentry effects, and buried hundreds of other bugs across flight, construction and rendering, including some severe VRAM leaks.
