We have just released the third snapshot of week 36 of 2026.

This snapshot adds mineable uranium deposits to Duna and Pol. Uranium can now be scanned from orbit, mined with drills and stored in solid resource containers.

It also adds stage-based fuel priorities and per-tank flow controls, alongside fixes for vessel control and momentum after destructive impacts, terrain recovery, and aerodynamic and thermal shielding inside closed fairings and cargo bays.
