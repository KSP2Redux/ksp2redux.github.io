We have just released the second snapshot for the 39th week of 2026.

This is a quick hotfix for [Snapshot 26w39a](/blog/snapshot-26w39a/). It fixes an issue with the Part Manager and the padding around some UIs, such as the Mission Control view.

As always, snapshots are in the beta channel of the [Redux Updater](/download/) and are in heavy development, so expect bugs and don't play your main saves on them. If you find any, report them in game with Ctrl+B, or Escape then Report Redux Bug.
