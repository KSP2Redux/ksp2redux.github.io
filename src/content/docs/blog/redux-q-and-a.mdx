Hello everyone! We've recently been getting a lot of questions about Redux - what new things you can expect in the future, how the roadmap features are going to work, and much more, so we have decided to hold a little Q&A session livestream on our Discord server (https://discord.gg/ksp2redux).

Specific date and time are going to be announced later, but you can generally expect it to take place in ~2 weeks at around [8 or 9pm UTC](https://dateful.com/convert/utc?t=20) in order to make it possible for players in most time zones to attend, and we will also record the session and put it up on our YouTube channel and website for anyone who missed it.

You can submit your questions in this form: https://forms.gle/r7VC6dWF9mqG4LJi7. First though, please read through https://ksp2redux.org/roadmap to see if your question is already answered there.

While we will try to also answer some questions on the fly, submitted questions will have higher priority.

Please, do not spam a ton of questions into one form - if you want to ask multiple unrelated things, you can submit the form multiple times, though we will try to be fair and answer as many people's questions as possible, rather than spend an hour reading a single person's questions, which I hope is understandable.
