This snapshot is primarily bugfixes; time-warp during burns is now fixed, so that burns with engines like the Dawn can keep going under time warp when there’s enough xenon and electricity, and auto-warp will speed back up after a temporary slowdown like an eclipse.

Additionally, cloud edges look cleaner when they are in front of vessels or the KSC, and distant clouds and atmospheres look better at shallow angles. Landing legs also cause less slowdown while supporting a vessel on the ground, and Free Flight now no longer blocks debug UI windows, and updates terrain and lighting correctly as the camera moves between planets and moons.

Many other smaller bugfixes made it into this release, so thank you everyone for your bug reports and playtesting!
